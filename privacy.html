Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This policy applies to the AliExpress Price Comparison application ("the App"), operated by its owner ("we"). Contact: snd.inquiry@gmail.com.

What the App does

The App compares AliExpress sellers and uploads the resulting videos to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.

Google user data we access

When the owner signs in with Google, the App requests the YouTube scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube. It is used only to upload videos, set their title and description, and confirm the channel name. The App does not read your emails, contacts, files or any other Google data.

How data is stored

The only Google data stored is an OAuth refresh token, kept privately in the owner's own Google Drive so the App does not ask for sign-in repeatedly. It is not stored on any other server.

Sharing

We do not sell, share, transfer or disclose Google user data to anyone. We do not use it for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deletion

You can revoke the App's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To have stored tokens deleted, email snd.inquiry@gmail.com.

Changes

We will update this page if the way the App uses Google user data changes.
