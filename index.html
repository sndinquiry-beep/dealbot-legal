AliExpress Price Comparison

AliExpress Price Comparison is a personal tool that compares sellers of the same or similar AliExpress products by price, rating, orders and delivery time, and shares the results as short videos and posts on our own channels.

The tool signs in to the owner's own YouTube channel, with the owner's permission, only to upload these comparison videos.
